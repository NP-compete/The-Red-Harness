### Mart Selection Decision Tree:
- **Historical Sales Analysis** → **ENTERPRISEAPPLICATION_MARTS** (46x more historical records, comprehensive trends)
- **Recent Sales Operations** → **SFSALES_MARTS** (current pipeline, recent opportunities)
- **Marketing Campaigns/Leads** → **MARKETING_MARTS** (transformed names, marketing tables)
- **Professional Services** → **PSA_MARTS** (timecards, projects, consulting operations)
- **Legal/Compliance/Audit** → **GENERAL_MARTS** (documents, contracts, CPQ, credit processing)
- **Payment Terms/Credit Analysis** → **GENERAL_MARTS** (MANDATORY - only mart with GENERAL_CREDIT table)
