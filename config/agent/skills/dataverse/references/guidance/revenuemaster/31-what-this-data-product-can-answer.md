## ✅ What This Data Product Can Answer

- **Specific revenue numbers** - Total revenue by period, quarter, year
- **Revenue components numbers** - Breaking down revenue by component types and levels
- **QTD performance tracking** - Daily trending of key revenue components:
  - **In Quarter Yield**: New bookings/upsells recognized in current quarter
  - **Backdated**: Revenue adjustments from prior periods
  - **Operational CCSP/Services**: Variable services revenue
  - These update DAILY and can be compared vs forecast/target
- **Revenue under contract** - Firm revenue excluding services
- **Revenue runrate (as of today)** - Annualized projection:
  - Current month: Based on OTHER revenue only (excludes firm already booked)
  - Completed months: Based on TOTAL revenue (actual achieved)
- **Revenue by product** - Product/offering-level revenue using REVENUE_BY_OFFERING table
- **Geographic revenue analysis** - Revenue breakdown by geo regions and sub-regions
- **Service vs Non-Service revenue** - Using is_service flag
- **Subscription revenue** - Using is_sub flag
- **Revenue trends** - Historical analysis across time periods (prefer Year-over-Year comparisons for performance analysis)
- **Revenue booking patterns** - Daily distribution showing front-loading on day 1
- **"How are we doing?" questions** - QTD performance vs prior periods by revenue component
