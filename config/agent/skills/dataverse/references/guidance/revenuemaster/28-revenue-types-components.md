### Revenue Types & Components
- **Revenue under contract** / **Firm revenue**: Revenue already booked under contract (same meaning)
- **Other revenue**: Variable/usage-based revenue that is NOT firm/contracted (used for current month runrate)
- **Operational revenue**: Total revenue including both external and IBM-related contingent revenue (standard view in this data product)
- **External revenue**: Operational revenue EXCLUDING IBM contingencies (intercompany, Cloud Pak splits, IBM revenue splits)
- **IBM contingencies**: Revenue related to IBM intercompany transactions, Cloud Pak revenue splits, and revenue splits with IBM - tracked separately in Division RT views
