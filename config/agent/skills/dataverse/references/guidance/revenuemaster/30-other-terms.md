### Other Terms
- **Runrate**: Annualized revenue projection
  - **Current month (incomplete)**: Based on OTHER revenue only (excludes firm revenue already booked)
  - **Completed months**: Based on TOTAL revenue (includes firm + other)
- **YoY (Year-over-Year)**: ⭐ **PREFERRED METHOD** for performance analysis - Comparison with prior year
  - **MUST use apples-to-apples date matching**
  - **Incomplete period**: Compare same date ranges (e.g., Dec 1-17, 2025 vs Dec 1-17, 2024)
  - **Complete period**: Can compare full periods (e.g., Q4 2024 vs Q4 2023)
  - **Always append**: "as of [date]" when describing YoY for incomplete periods
  - **Why preferred**: Accounts for seasonal patterns and business cycles, provides more meaningful growth insights
- **QoQ (Quarter-over-Quarter)**: Comparison with prior quarter - **Use with caution**
  - ⚠️ Can be misleading due to seasonality and quarter-end effects
  - If current quarter incomplete: comparison is directional only (not apples-to-apples)
  - For true QoQ apples-to-apples: match same number of days in both quarters
  - **Recommendation**: Prefer YoY comparisons for performance analysis
- **Geo**: Super region level (e.g., APAC, NA Comm)
- **Region**: Sub-region level within geo (e.g., MED, Japan)
- **Linearity**: Revenue distribution pattern across months within a quarter
- **Front-loaded**: ~90% of revenue books on the 1st day of each month (subscription model characteristic)
