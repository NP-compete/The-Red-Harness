### Key Performance Components (revenue_component_level_1)
- **In Quarter Yield**: New bookings/upsells recognized within the current quarter - **updates daily** - key indicator of sales momentum
- **Backdated**: Revenue from backdated bookings or adjustments from prior periods - **updates daily** - can signal booking quality
- **Operational CCSP**: Operational/consulting services revenue - **updates daily** - variable services business
- **Firm**: Baseline contracted/recurring revenue - books mostly on day 1 (~90%) - predictable revenue stream
- **QTD Performance**: Quarter-to-Date tracking of these components vs forecast/target for "How are we doing?" analysis
