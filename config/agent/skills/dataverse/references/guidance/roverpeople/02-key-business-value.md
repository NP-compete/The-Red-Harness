### Key Business Value
- **Employee Directory**: Comprehensive employee and contractor information management
- **Organizational Structure**: Manager hierarchies and reporting relationships
- **Product/Role Alignment**: Employee assignments to products and organizational roles
- **Workforce Analytics**: Employee demographics, tenure analysis, and organizational insights
- **Tax Credit Management**: Employee tax credit assignments and product alignment tracking
- **Geographic Distribution**: Global workforce location and regional management
