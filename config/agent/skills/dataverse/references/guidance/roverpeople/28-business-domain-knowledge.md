## 🔍 Business Domain Knowledge

### Employee Types & Classification
- **Full-time Employees**: Regular Red Hat employees with standard benefits and long-term employment
- **Contingent Workers**: Contractors, consultants, and temporary workers with specific project assignments

### Red Hat Product Ecosystem
- **Core Products**: Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL), OpenShift, Ansible, JBoss, Satellite
- **Cloud Products**: OpenShift Container Platform, Red Hat OpenStack Platform, CloudForms
- **Middleware**: JBoss Enterprise Application Platform, Red Hat Fuse, Red Hat AMQ
- **Management Tools**: Satellite, Insights, Subscription Management, Ansible Tower

### Organizational Structure
- **Engineering Functions**: Product development, quality assurance, documentation, release engineering
- **Support Functions**: Technical support, customer success, professional services
- **Business Functions**: Marketing, sales, finance, human resources, legal
