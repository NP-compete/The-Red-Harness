### **IntelligentAccess**
**Database:** `INTELLIGENTACCESS_DB`
**Schema:** `MARTS`
**Maturity:** AI Experimental

The Intelligent Access data product provides subscription line–level detail and aggregated entitlement views by unifying data from Subscriptions, Orders, MDM Product, CIAM, EBS, Bookings Master, and Enterprise Shared. It supports customer identity, account history, subscription entitlements, product attributes, orders, opportunities (where available), and sales account/sales group segmentation for Supportable 360 (legacy customer/subscription view).

**Primary use cases:** Enables retrieval of active and historical entitlements with associated accounting value, searchable by EBS account number (primary), Global Sales Group ID/name, Sales Group ID/name, Order Number, Installation Number, or Subscription Number. Supports renewal preparation, remix/upgrade identification, gap analysis between entitlements and expected active terms, and SKU-level entitlement visibility.
