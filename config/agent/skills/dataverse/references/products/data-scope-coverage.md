### Data Scope & Coverage

* **Data Included:**

  * Subscription line details (parent and child SKU)

  * Subscription entitlements and associated accounting value

  * Customer and account identity (EBS account, Sales Group, Global Sales Group)

  * Product attributes (SKU/MDM); orders and opportunities (where available)

  * Sales account and sales group segmentation

* **Data Excluded (Critical Guardrail):**

  * Full PII (e.g., unmasked contact details)

  * Internal cost or margin data

  * Support cases, tickets, or incidents; consumption, usage, or telemetry data

  * Invoice, payment, or AR transactional data; opportunity pipeline, forecast, or booking data

  * Renewal quotes or renewal opportunity data; product catalog or master data not tied to subscriptions
