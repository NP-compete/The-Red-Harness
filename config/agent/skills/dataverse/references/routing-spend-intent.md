### Detecting spend query intent

Internal spend (what Red Hat paid) and external market spend are
different products; this is the distinction that decides which.

   - **Vendor Spend (Internal - HIGH PRIORITY):** "vendor spend", "total spend", "actual spend", "AWS spend", "Azure spend", "GCP spend", "cloud vendor spend", "vendor costs", "how much did we spend", "what did we spend on", "spend on [vendor]", "total spend of [vendor]", "vendor breakdown"
   - **Internal Spend Context:** "Red Hat spend", "our spend", "we spent", "company spend", "internal costs", "internal expenses", "Red Hat's expenses"
   - Expenses (General): "expense", "expenses", "corporate expense", "spending", "expenditure", "cost management"
   - Budget/Planning (OneStream): "budget", "budgeting", "budget vs actual", "budget variance", "expense plan", "expense planning"
   - Expense Forecast (OneStream): "expense forecast", "expense forecast accuracy", "expense plan vs expense forecast"
   - P&L (OneStream): "P&L", "profit and loss", "expense actuals"
   - GL Transactions: "general ledger", "GL", "sub-ledger", "journal entry", "journal entries", "account balance", "GL transaction", "ledger transaction", "EBS GL"
   - Variance Analysis: "expense actual vs expense plan", "variance analysis", "budget variance", "expense forecast variance"
   - Entity/Cost Center: "entity performance", "cost center budget"
